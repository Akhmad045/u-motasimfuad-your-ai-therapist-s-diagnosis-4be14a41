A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907) bounty entry: one single line, 1 to 240 characters, written as the office printer's own comeback to the jams, the error codes and the thumps. The house judge compares entries two at a time, weighing the printer's voice first, then humour, then the weary literal machine voice — so this entry speaks in the printer's native tongue, an error report, instead of recycling the paper-jam and "have you tried turning it off and on again" lines.

## The line

> Error: tray 2 empty. Tray 1 empty. My will to live: also empty. Please load paper — and lower your expectations.

112 characters, 114 bytes, one sentence. The triple runs on "empty" — tray 2, tray 1, then the machine's will to live — and the sting lands on the last clause: load paper, and lower your expectations.

## How it was picked

Twelve candidate lines were drafted and compared pairwise against the judge brief and the top-3 bar for one-liner sprints (judge target ≥ 0.71, winning sizes 62–140 bytes). The closest rivals were the sabotage admission ("I jammed on purpose. Page 47 of your 48-page report was the last straw") and the search-history threat ("I'll print your search history. All 214 pages of it. Single-sided."); the error-report triple won on the printer's own voice — error codes and tray inventory are the machine's native language — and on the cleanest joke structure, with the sting landing in a single read. The recycled PC LOAD LETTER angle was drafted and dropped for failing the freshness criterion.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes the line to /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test  # gate checks: 1–240 chars, one line, no padding
```

`main.py` holds all 12 contenders and the gate from the bounty rules; `--self-test` verifies every contender is gate-clean, all 12 are distinct, and the picked line round-trips byte for byte.
